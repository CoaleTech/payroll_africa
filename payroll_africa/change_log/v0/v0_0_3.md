# Payroll Africa 0.0.3

Full 54-country coverage — every country now ships with configurable statutory settings, verified rates, and an Espresso-aligned UI.

## New
- Per-country setup for the remaining regions: Central Africa (Cameroon, Gabon, Congo, CAR, Chad, Equatorial Guinea, Sao Tome), North Africa (Algeria, Libya, Sudan, Mauritania), East Africa (Djibouti, Eritrea, Comoros, Seychelles), Franco-West (Senegal, Mali, Niger, Burkina Faso, Benin, Togo, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau), West-Anglo (Sierra Leone, Liberia, Gambia, Cabo Verde, Mauritius) and Zimbabwe.
- All 54 countries are now provisioned with configurable Settings DocTypes and tax bands; 44 ship pre-built Income Tax Slabs and Salary Structure templates.
- Togo INAM health contribution (employee + employer) with new settings fields.
- Documented no-op handling for Somalia and South Sudan (no mandatory statutory deductions).

## Improved
- Statutory rate corrections across ~30 countries, verified against PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries and national revenue authorities (e.g. Kenya NSSF ceiling, Zambia NAPSA cap, Egypt exemption, Tunisia CNSS, Morocco IR bands, Algeria IRG, Namibia SSC, Botswana PAYE, Burkina/Benin/Togo contribution ceilings and PIT bands).
- 16 additional currency records added for the newly seeded countries.
- Change Log ("What's New") dialog restyled to the Frappe Espresso design system — all colors, typography, radii and shadows use Espresso tokens, with automatic light/dark theme support.
- Salary Slip Africa Standard print format aligned to the Espresso palette and Inter typography (PDF-safe literal values).

## Fixed
- Burkina Faso, Benin and Togo contribution ceilings and progressive PIT/IUTS/IRPP bands corrected.
- Namibia SSC annual ceiling synced between setup defaults and live settings.
- Change Log dialog dark-mode colors no longer hardcoded — badges and banner adapt to the active theme.

## Technical
- 347 unit tests passing.
- All changed files ruff-clean.
